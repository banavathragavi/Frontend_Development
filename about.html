About Lenovo



Lenovo is a global technology company that designs, develops, and manufactures innovative products including laptops, desktops, tablets, smartphones, servers, and smart devices. Founded in 1984 in Beijing, China, Lenovo has grown from a small startup into one of the world's leading technology brands.

The company is widely recognized for its high-quality ThinkPad, IdeaPad, Legion, and Yoga series of laptops. Lenovo focuses on delivering cutting-edge technology, reliability, and performance to students, professionals, gamers, and businesses worldwide.

In 2005, Lenovo expanded its global presence by acquiring IBM's Personal Computer Division, which helped establish the company as a major player in the international technology market.

Today, Lenovo operates in more than 180 countries and continues to innovate in areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Data Centers, and Smart Devices. Its mission is to create smarter technology that empowers people and transforms the way they work, learn, and connect.
